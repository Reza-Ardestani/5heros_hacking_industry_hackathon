# Product discovery — Bottleneck Busters

Research checked October 3, 2026. This is an evidence-backed product hypothesis,
not validated product-market fit. The reported family connection was clarified:
the person does not work in the relevant department. No direct practitioner
interview, customer endorsement, or example funding decision has been established.

## Decision for the hackathon

Keep the working corridor experiment. Position it as an auditable study workflow
for transportation engineers, with a recommendation handoff to a budget reviewer.
Test whether constrained revision and report preparation reduce repeat work.
Do not pitch absence of engineering tools, random municipal decisions, citywide
bottleneck ranking, or an infrastructure maintenance optimizer.

## Evidence maturity

- **Public facts:** primary City, vendor, and government sources in the evidence ledger.
- **Prototype evidence:** source code and recorded SUMO output demonstrate a local
  workflow on a synthetic corridor. They do not demonstrate customer demand.
- **Customer hypotheses:** repeated setup/reporting effort, review friction,
  pilot access, willingness to pay, and adoption constraints remain untested.
- **Field evidence:** observed corridor calibration and intervention effects absent.

Move a claim between these categories only when its supporting artifact exists.
No interviews were conducted or outreach sent during this desk research.
