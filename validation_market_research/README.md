Trevor's forwarded response provides practitioner feedback on decision justification, public impact, and transparent recommendations. These support product hypotheses; they do not establish City endorsement, buying authority, willingness to pay, or verified investment outcomes. The statistics and product capabilities in the question introduction remain claims made in that outreach draft.

The PDF is historical guidance from 2014. This archive does not establish that it is the current City standard.
