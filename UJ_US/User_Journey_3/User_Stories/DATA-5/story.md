# DATA-5 — Handle missing evidence and revise eligibility

Journey: [UJ-BB-003](../../journey.md).
Client: [ICP-BB-003](../../../Ideal_Client_Profiles/ICP_3_Data_Operations/profile.md).

As a planner with data steward, I want to see exact missing/stale evidence and permitted alternatives, so I can make a conditional decision or defer when evidence is insufficient.

Status: **Proposed resource-aware workflow**. This is not new customer validation.

## Acceptance

- Unknown availability remains unknown; established shortage rejects the affected option.
- Agent may propose bounded alternate sources or manual-import requests.
- No supplier contact, purchase, City request or equipment booking occurs without human authorization.
- Retiming is assessed against its own resource and engineering constraints.
