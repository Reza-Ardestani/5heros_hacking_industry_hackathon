## **1\. Welcome & Executive Summary**

Welcome to the IEEE Southern Alberta Section Young Professionals **Industry Hackathon** 2026\! This handbook serves as your complete guide for the weekend.  
Inside, you will find details regarding the 48-hour schedule, venue navigation at the University of Calgary, campus Wi-Fi access, transit options, team size limits, equipment requirements, GitHub submission procedures, presentation guidelines, sponsorship and partner perks, and safety/ethical standards. Please review this handbook thoroughly.

## **2\. What to build?**

**Theme:** Autonomous Intelligence for Industrial Innovation

While many hackathons focus on building surface-level productivity apps or basic chat interfaces, our mission is to deliver technical value that addresses **deep "hard science" and heavy-engineering bottlenecks**. We are looking for solutions that move beyond "productivity gains" and instead provide technical value. The most critical challenges in modern industry are technical bottlenecks rooted in the massive complexity of physical, mathematical, and structural problems. This hackathon is strictly focused on creating **a valuable solution** capable of reasoning through complex data, managing simulations, automating engineering design loops, analyzing chemical/material properties, or optimizing physical systems (e.g., predicting grid frequency anomalies or optimizing manufacturing cycles).

## **10\. Judging Criteria, Pitch Structure & Demo Requirements**

**Presentation Format & Time Limit:**

> * Each team is allotted a total of **8 minutes** in front of the judges:   
  * **5 minutes presentation \+ 3 minutes Q\&A**.  
> * **In-Person Software Demo Requirement:** Presentations must be delivered in person. While teams are welcome to build slide decks, it is strongly advised to showcase the **actual working software/AI agent live** in front of judges rather than relying solely on slides (this is not a case competition).  
> * **Judging Rubric:** Review the official rubric on GitHub: [GitHub Judging Rubric](https://github.com/nagusubra/industry-hackathon-lab/blob/main/JUDGING_RUBRIC.md).

**5-Minute Pitch Structure (Rule of Thumb for Maximum Impact):**

> * **Introduction (5 \- 10 seconds):** Briefly introduce yourself and your team in a memorable way.  
> * **Problem Statement (1 minute):** Clearly describe the problem you are addressing, who benefits, and the tangible impact.  
> * **Software Demo (1 \- 2 minutes):** Explain the technical architecture, decision-making process, and key engineering choices without overwhelming details. Live demonstration of your working software/agent performing autonomous tasks or solving the target problem.  
> * **Wrap-up (1 \- 2 minutes):** Summarize key technical takeaways and end with a strong closing statement.

## **14\. Hackathon Eligibility criteria, Safety and AI Ethics**

All attendees must maintain high standards of professional behavior, respect, and academic integrity.

> * Treat all participants, mentors, volunteers, and staff with respect. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited.  
> * All code submitted must be original or properly attribute open-source framework baselines.  
> * Destructive network testing, scraping, or unauthorized system access is prohibited.

Hackathon Eligibility criteria:

1. Age and gender: Anyone above the legal age (18+) is welcome to join, whether you're a seasoned coder, a curious creator, or just passionate about AI and innovation\!  
2. Profiles: Anyone is encouraged to participate in the hackathon whether you are a first year university student, a recent graduate, a young professional or a seasoned professional.  
3. Team size: Minimum team size of 2 members required. You can participate as a team of up to 5 members.  
4. Submission requirements: Code with the clock\! All development must happen during the official hackathon window. Submit your masterpiece by the deadline. Late entries won’t be accepted, so set your reminders\!

### **AI & Ethics**

Since the theme of this year's Hackathon is Artificial Intelligence (AI), please keep in mind that it is a rapidly evolving field. AI and machine learning (ML) models are only as good as the data given to them and the skilled expertise that guides it. When building AI solutions, carefully consider how algorithmic biases could impact the solutions built during the hackathon, and ensure responsible, transparent agent design.

### **3\. Code of Conduct & Behavioral Standards** {#3.-code-of-conduct-&-behavioral-standards}

The IEEE Industry Hackathon is committed to offering a safe, inclusive, welcoming, and professional environment for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, age, or religion.

> * **Respectful Conduct:** Treat all participants, mentors, judges, venue staff, and volunteers with professional courtesy and respect. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, stalking, offensive comments, or unwanted physical contact will not be tolerated in-person or on Discord.  
> * **Academic & Build Integrity:** All submissions must represent original work created during the hackathon window. Any pre-existing open-source code, libraries, or datasets utilized must be clearly disclosed and cited in your GitHub submission.  
> * **Cyber Security & Fair Play:** Participants must not execute destructive network testing, scraping, denial-of-service attacks, password probing, or unauthorized system access against university networks, sponsor APIs, or fellow participants' devices.  
> * **Sponsor API Guidelines:** You are encouraged to use sponsor-provided API keys and credits (such as ElevenLabs Creator/Pro coupons) strictly for hackathon prototyping in accordance with published terms. Do not share credentials publicly or exfiltrate private data.  
> * **Venue Etiquette:** Respect University of Calgary Hunter Hub facilities. Keep aisles clear, maintain food discipline, dispose of waste properly, and assist with standard room resets on Friday and Sunday.
